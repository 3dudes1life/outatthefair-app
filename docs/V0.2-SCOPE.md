# OATF App V0.2 Scope

## Design upgrade

V0.2 translates the rebuilt OATF website into a mobile app language rather than shrinking the desktop website. It uses the same dark editorial mood, aurora color, bold condensed headlines, italic accent typography, rainbow signal line and premium card system.

## New in V0.2

1. Editorial “All Belong at the Fair” home experience
2. Fair-day live dashboard with Happening Now and Up Next
3. Global search across fairs, performers, organizations, services and history
4. Richer fair profiles with OATF website, directions and official fair links
5. Expanded six-step digital passport
6. Larger text, higher contrast and reduced-motion preferences
7. Upgraded schedule, favorites and My OATF presentation
8. Improved community, performer and map experiences
9. Updated PWA metadata, cache and shortcuts
10. V0.2 data model for announcements, website links and passport challenges
